Frequently Asked Questions

Ordinary computers are transformed into powerful communication hubs by Asterisk PBX. It is open source software which lets anyone create even complex phone system, video platform, conferencing solution, etc. using the internet technology (VoIP) instead of the traditional phone lines. Asterisk runs on standard servers, and acts as a digital switchboard, routing calls, handling conferences, and voice messages. Its flexibility shines through customizable features: IVR allows businesses to design call menus (IVR), set complex routing rules, record conversations, and create unique voice applications designed for them.

Asterisk VoIP is software based phone system that relies on internet protocols to accomplish voice communications. It converts traditional phone calls into digital signals that travel over IP networks, enabling features like:

  • Voice and video calling
  • Conference bridging
  • Voicemail
  • Call queuing and routing
  • Interactive Voice Response (IVR) systems

Our Asterisk development services includes:

  • Custom communication solutions using the Asterisk platform.
  • Updating existing features and functionalities through programming.
  • Asterisk integration with other business systems
  • Custom dial plans and call flows
  • Creating custom IVR menus and voice applications
  • Asterisk server setup and configuration.

Asterisk PBX and Asterisk VoIP are similar but different things in the communication system. Asterisk PBX is the complete phone system software that provides all the communications functions, managing calls, routing, and voicemail and call conferencing, all from a central hub. In other words, it’s ‘the brain’ of the whole phone system. On the other hand, Asterisk VoIP refers to the technology within Asterisk PBX that deals with voice communication over an internet protocol. VoIP is one of the main abilities that Asterisk PBX uses to pass on voice data over networks. Asterisk PBX will function with traditional phone lines, but Asterisk VoIP is concerned with the internet voice transmission side of things. Asterisk VoIP is just how Asterisk PBX handles internet based voice communications; Asterisk PBX is the entire system.
