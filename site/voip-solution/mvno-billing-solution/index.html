Frequently Asked Questions

A Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) is a wireless service provider that rents network infrastructure from an existing network operator and markets a digital communication server to his targeted audience. An MVNO provider offers services like voice, data, and messaging to customers. MVNOs focus on branding, marketing, and customer service while leveraging the network of established carriers, often at lower costs, to provide customized mobile plans.

An MVNO billing platform is a cloud-based solution that manages the complete billing process for MVNOs. Our MVNO billing system handles real-time customer usage tracking, applies pricing models, generates invoices, processes payments, and provides detailed reports.

The major types of MVNO businesses include: Full MVNO: Operates its own core network but leases the radio access from an MNO. Light MVNO: Relies more heavily on the MNO’s infrastructure and focuses on customer acquisition, branding, and marketing. Branded Reseller: Doesn’t handle technical operations, relying entirely on the MNO for all backend functions while focusing on marketing and sales.

Our MVNO billing platform is versatile and designed to manage a wide range of business aspects, including: Customer onboarding, Real-time rating and charging of usage, Invoicing and payment processing, Revenue management and data analysis, Fraud detection and prevention, Multiple pricing models (prepaid, postpaid, hybrid).

The MVNO billing system helps prevent fraud by tracking live calls and monitoring, which detects unusual or suspicious activity immediately. With automated alerts for abnormal usage patterns or unauthorized access, operators can quickly intervene to prevent fraudulent behavior. Our MVNO billing software also offers secure payment processing, fraud detection algorithms, and historical data analysis to identify trends that may indicate potential fraud, minimizing financial risks for the business.

Emerging trends in MVNO billing solutions include: Real-time billing and usage monitoring and it ensures an accurate and immediate charge calculation. Flexible, customer-centric pricing models, such as subscription-based plans or pay-as-you-go options. Cloud-based billing platforms that offer scalability, cost-efficiency, and remote accessibility. Convergence billing that enables bundling of multiple services (e.g., mobile, internet, and TV) into a single invoice for a seamless experience.
