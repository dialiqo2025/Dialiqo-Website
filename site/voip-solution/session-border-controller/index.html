Protect Your VoIP Communications with Our Advanced SBC Solutions

Deliver secure, seamless and real-time VoIP communications with our reliable session border controller. A session border controller is a dedicated piece of hardware or software application that governs how VoIP communications over a network are initiated, transmitted and terminated. They are deployed at network borders, acting as gatekeepers between VoIP networks and carrier networks to monitor the VoIP data as it flows through these networks.

SBC can also act as a firewall for session traffic. It continuously monitors incoming and outgoing traffic to detect any spam, malicious or unauthorized access attempts. Originally intended to control and protect VoIP networks, Dialiqo’s SBCs are now extensively used to inspect all kinds of real-time communications, such as text messages, IP video and collaboration sessions.

How Our Session Border Controller Works

SBCs are installed at the border of the organization’s network that protect its users from external threats and network attacks.Our SBCs support interoperability, meaning they can facilitate connections between different vendors by translating various codecs and protocols. Precisely, SBCs can convert data numerous dialects of SIP to make sure multiple systems can smoothly communicate with each other.

SBCs are also responsible for creating connections between various voice and video codecs, including g.729, g.711 and H.323. They can also transcode between lower and higher quality bandwidths to match the capabilities of the current network infrastructure. This is especially beneficial when connections are taking place over a complex and multi-vendor network, ensuring seamless communication sessions.
